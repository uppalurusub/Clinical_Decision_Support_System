Sample Request 4 — Minimal / Incomplete Information

This is particularly useful for testing your Missing Information Agent and Critic Agent.

{
  "patient_id": "PAT-10004",
  "symptoms": {
    "chief_complaint": "Headache",
    "duration": "Unknown",
    "severity": 7,
    "associated_symptoms": [
      "Nausea"
    ]
  },
  "history": {
    "age": 45
  },
  "vitals": {},
  "medications": [],
  "allergies": [],
  "labs": [],
  "imaging": []
}

Your workflow should not assume that missing vitals, neurological examination, medication history, or imaging means they are normal.

The MissingInformationAgent should potentially identify things such as:

Missing:
- Blood pressure
- Temperature
- Neurological examination
- Headache onset
- Sudden vs gradual onset
- History of trauma
- Medication/substance use
- Visual symptoms
- Focal neurological symptoms
